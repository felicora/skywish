# SkyWish 🏮✨

SkyWish is a cozy and interactive web experience where visitors write a wish, personalize a glowing lantern, light it by hand, and watch it drift into the night sky.

Designed to create a peaceful moment of reflection and hope, SkyWish combines atmospheric visuals, gentle animations, ambient music, and meaningful interactions inspired by traditional sky lantern festivals.

## Features

- 📝 Write a personal wish
- 🎨 Customize lantern colors and blessing symbols
- 🔥 Light your lantern through an interactive drag-and-release experience
- 🌌 Watch your lantern float into the stars
- 🌙 Explore beautiful skies, ambient music, moonlight, mist, and shooting stars
- 🎨Change the wallpers and layouts to your choice
- 🏮 Build your own persistent Wish Sky where released lanterns are remembered
- 💫 Revisit, read, and manage past wishes
- 🎵 Relax with calming soundscapes and music options
- ♿ Responsive, keyboard-friendly, and accessibility-focused design

## The Experience

1. Choose the atmosphere and music for your night.
2. Write a wish from the heart.
3. Customize your lantern.
4. Light the lantern and let it glow.
5. Release it into the sky.
6. Watch it join your growing collection of wishes among the stars.

**Light a wish. Let it rise. Let the sky remember.** 🌠
